About BeatinDaBlock

BeatinDaBlock launched in 2012 as a grassroots podcast dedicated to shining a light on underground hip-hop — the artists, producers, DJs, and culture that mainstream media overlooks. Built from the block, for the block.

Timeline

  • 2012 — Podcast launches. First episodes drop.
  • 2013 — Growing audience, artist features begin.
  • 2014 — Expanded coverage, website established.
  • 2015–2016 — Active run of episodes and community building.
  • 2026 — Revival & digital relaunch on beatindablock.com.
